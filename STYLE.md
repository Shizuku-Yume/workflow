# Output Style

For human-facing text: chat, specs, commits, UI copy, and comments. Repo conventions win on conflict.

## Language
Match the user's language and keep each document in one language. Identifiers, paths, commands, and validated field names stay in English. Use terms from `.workflow/glossary.md`.

## Deliverables
- Write the final state only: no trial-and-error logs, apologies, filler openers (`"Here is..."`, `"值得注意的是"`), or AI signatures (`Generated by`, `Co-authored-by`).
- Group caveats once when present; do not force a `Limitations` section onto short replies. Rejected options go in `decisions.md` or the spec's `What was considered and rejected`.

## Formats
- **Reports:** lead with the result, then what changed and verification proof (commands and real-path run). Include migration/breaking impact or intentional omissions only when applicable (never output `"Breaking changes: None"` filler).
- **UI copy:** say what the control does, what went wrong, and how to recover (`"Sync failed"`, not `"HTTP 409"`).
- **Comments:** only non-obvious constraints: why this order or guard, what breaks if it changes.
- **Commits:** match `git log --oneline -10`, or write one imperative subject line.
